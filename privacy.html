Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

This policy explains what postfarmapp (the "Service") collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account connection data from each platform you connect: your account ID, username, display name and profile picture, plus the access and refresh tokens the platform issues.
  • Content you provide: videos, captions and schedules you create in the Service.
  • Post data: the IDs, links, status and public performance numbers (such as views and likes) of posts the Service publishes for you.

We do not collect your platform passwords, and we do not read your private messages.

How we use it

  • To publish and schedule the posts you ask for, on the accounts you connected.
  • To show you your accounts, queue, history and post performance.
  • To keep connections working, for example by refreshing tokens.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the platform you are publishing to.

TikTok

When you connect TikTok, the Service uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API with the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish, only to identify your account and to upload or publish the videos you choose.

Storage and security

Data is stored on private servers. Tokens are encrypted at rest and access is restricted.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is connected. When you disconnect an account, its tokens are deleted. You can also revoke access at any time in the platform's own settings (for example TikTok, Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions). To have all of your data deleted, contact us and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above shows the latest version.
