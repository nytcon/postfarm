postfarmapp

Schedule and publish your own short videos to your social accounts from one dashboard.

postfarmapp is a private publishing tool. You connect your own TikTok, Instagram, X and YouTube accounts through each platform's official sign-in, then upload, schedule and publish videos you made to those accounts.

  • Accounts connect through official OAuth. Your passwords are never seen or stored.
  • Every post goes through the platform's official publishing API.
  • You choose what is posted, where and when, and you can disconnect an account at any time.

Read the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy. Questions: github.com/nytcon/postfarm/issues.
